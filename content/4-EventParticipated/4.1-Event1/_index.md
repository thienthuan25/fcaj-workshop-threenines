# Event Report: "First Cloud AI Journey Community Day"

- Time: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, March 21, 2026.
- Floor 26, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 2 Hai Trieu Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

### Purpose of the Event

**FCAJ Community Day** is a technology event organized to connect the community interested in cloud computing, DevOps, Artificial Intelligence, and more. The event provides participants with the opportunity to explore the latest technology trends, listen to insights shared by industry experts, and learn about modern solutions being implemented on the **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** platform.

In addition to technical content, the event also introduced company culture, career orientation, internship opportunities, and provided participants with the opportunity to network and communicate directly with the speakers and others who share the same career interests in the technology field.

### Participation Role

I attended the event as a participant. Throughout the program, I mainly listened to presentations from the speakers, followed the technical sessions, and gained new knowledge related to AWS, DevOps, Platform Engineering, and Generative AI. Through the event, I had the opportunity to gain a deeper understanding of the technology trends currently being adopted by enterprises as well as career development directions for the future.

### Content

The event was divided into multiple sessions covering different topics, focusing on modern technologies built on the AWS platform.

#### Topic 1: Building Modern Platform Engineering & Career Pathways

The first session introduced **Platform Engineering**, a trend that is being widely adopted by enterprises to standardize infrastructure and software development processes. The speaker explained the role of Platform Engineering within the **Cloud** and **DevOps** ecosystem while also introducing career pathways and internship opportunities for students and young engineers.

#### Topic 2: GenAIOps Essential – DevOps for Generative AI Applications

This session focused on building and operating **Generative AI** applications on AWS. It introduced DevOps principles for AI, methods for deploying Generative AI models in real-world environments, and several representative services such as **Amazon Bedrock**, **Amazon EKS**, **Langfuse**, and **AgentCore Observability**.

This topic demonstrated that the combination of AI and DevOps is becoming an important trend in the development of modern AI systems.

#### Topic 3: Shipping Code in the Agentic Era

The speaker discussed the transformation of the software development process as **AI Agents** become increasingly popular. The session covered the challenges of developing AI-powered applications, developer tools, productivity improvement strategies, and a live demonstration showing how AI can assist software development.

#### Topic 4: Production-Grade Multimodal GenAI on AWS

This was one of the most outstanding sessions of the event. The speaker introduced the architecture for building enterprise-scale Generative AI systems, including:

- Modern AI application architecture.
- Multimodal Search with **Nova Embeddings**.
- GraphRAG for enterprise knowledge management.
- Multi-Agent Workflows.
- Solutions to ensure the safety, observability, and operation of AI systems.

This session helped participants understand that building enterprise AI applications involves much more than language models and requires multiple components to ensure efficiency and scalability.

#### Topic 5: From Edge To Origin: CloudFront as Your Foundation

The final session focused on **Amazon CloudFront** and the role of **CDN** in content delivery.

The main topics included:

- CloudFront for various types of workloads.
- Cost optimization with CloudFront.
- Security capabilities.
- Improved system reliability.
- Enhanced application performance through **Edge Locations**.

### What I Learned

After attending the event, I gained the following knowledge:

- A better understanding of **Platform Engineering** and its role in modern enterprises.
- An understanding of the trend of integrating **DevOps** with **Generative AI** through the **GenAIOps** model.
- Exposure to new AI technologies on AWS such as **Amazon Bedrock**, **Nova Embeddings**, **GraphRAG**, and **Multi-Agent**.
- A deeper understanding of the process of building, deploying, and operating enterprise-scale AI systems.
- Knowledge of the benefits of Amazon CloudFront in accelerating content delivery, improving reliability, optimizing costs, and enhancing application security.
- A clearer understanding of the skills that companies are seeking for Cloud Engineer, DevOps Engineer, and AI Engineer positions.

### Event Experience

The event was professionally organized, with well-structured content that made it easy for participants to follow each session. The venue on the 26th floor of Bitexco Financial Tower provided a modern environment that was well suited for learning and networking.

The speakers delivered their presentations clearly, combining fundamental concepts, practical experience, and live demonstrations, making it easier for participants to understand how these technologies are applied in real-world enterprises. I also had the opportunity to explore many emerging trends in **Cloud Computing**, **DevOps**, and **Generative AI**, which further motivated me to continue learning and developing my skills.

From my perspective, this was a valuable event because it not only provided technical knowledge but also gave me a clearer career direction in the fields of cloud computing and artificial intelligence.
